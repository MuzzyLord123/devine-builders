How Phil approaches groundworks

Everything above ground is only as good as what’s underneath it. Phil handles site clearance, excavation, foundations and footings, underpinning, drainage installation and repairs, concrete slabs, kerbing and trenching, the unglamorous work a solid build depends on.

It’s also the stage where honest advice matters most. Ground conditions vary street to street around Deeside, and Phil will tell you plainly what your site needs, not more, not less, before any concrete is poured.

FAQ

Groundworks questions, answered honestly

How much do groundworks cost?

Groundworks pricing depends on the dig, the ground conditions and access, so there are no fixed price lists. Every estimate is free and comes with no obligation. Tell Phil what you have in mind (photos help). He usually replies within a day or two, and comes back with a clear, fair price you can plan around once he's seen the job.

Do you cover my area?

Devine Builders is based in Connah's Quay and works across Flintshire and the wider North Wales area, including Shotton, Queensferry, Hawarden, Ewloe, Buckley, Mold, Flint and Deeside. If you're nearby and not sure, just ask and Phil will let you know either way.

Can you sort drainage problems?

Yes. Drainage installation and drain repairs are part of the groundworks Phil takes on, from a new run for an extension to fixing a persistent soggy patch or blocked gully. He'll find the cause before proposing the fix.
