FAQ

Extensions questions, answered honestly

How much does extension work cost?

Extension costs depend on the size, the spec and the groundwork involved, so there are no fixed price lists — every quote is free and comes with no obligation. Tell Phil what you have in mind (photos help) — he usually replies within a day or two, and comes back with a clear, fair price you can plan around once he's seen the job.

Do you cover my area?

Devine Builders is based in Connah's Quay and works across Flintshire and the wider North Wales area — including Shotton, Queensferry, Hawarden, Ewloe, Buckley, Mold, Flint and Deeside. If you're nearby and not sure, just ask and Phil will let you know either way.

Do I need planning permission for an extension?

It depends on the size and position of what you want to build — some projects fall under permitted development and others need an application. Every property is different, so Phil will give you straight, honest advice on what your project involves and point you in the right direction before anything is agreed.
